Job Analysis and Job Evaluation framework split by Technical, Operational, and Sales categories

How to use this framework: Classify each role before scoring. The category determines the measurement lens and the likely grade anchor.
Technical
Specialist / Expert roles
Roles where the primary value is domain expertise, solution design, or system architecture. Measure by solution quality and knowledge depth.
Operational
Process / Delivery roles
Roles that ensure consistent execution, compliance, and process excellence. Measure by throughput, accuracy, and SLA adherence.
Sales
Revenue / Client roles
Roles that directly influence pipeline, conversion, or revenue. Measure by win rate, deal size, and pipeline contribution.

Classification decision tree

Ask thisIf yesIf no
Does the role own or design a technical solution?TechnicalContinue
Does the role directly own revenue, win rate, or a client relationship?SalesContinue
Is the primary output process adherence, delivery, or coordination?OperationalRe-examine scope

Hybrid roles

For a hybrid role, assign the primary category based on where 60% or more of accountability sits, then note the secondary category as context.
70% Technical + 30% Sales -> Technical anchor C4-C5 or D1 likely

Key questions for Hiring Manager

  • Is this role the final technical authority, or does a senior architect review the design?
  • Does the role translate client business needs into architecture independently?
  • Are the technical domains genuinely integrated, or siloed?
  • What happens if this person is unavailable: is there a backup or does delivery stop?

The grading question for hybrid roles

Accountability vs. guidance: before finalising a grade, ask whether this role is accountable for team outcomes, or guides others while remaining individually accountable. Getting this wrong can shift the grade band by one full level.
